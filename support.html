Frequently Asked Questions

How do I restore my purchases?

To restore your subscription or previous purchases, go to the Settings screen in the app and tap on "Restore Purchases". Ensure you are logged in with the same Apple ID used to make the original purchase.

Why is my document scan not working?

Please ensure you have granted camera permissions to the app. Also, try to scan documents in a well-lit environment and ensure the document fits within the frame.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. To cancel, go to your iPhone's Settings > [Your Name] > Subscriptions, select VisaEasePro, and tap "Cancel Subscription".
