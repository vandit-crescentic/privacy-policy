Frequently Asked Questions

Do you store my conversations on a server?

Your historical conversation data is stored locally on your device. We pass individual messages securely to our AI provider solely to generate the responses you see. We do not permanently store your chats on our servers or use them to train models. For more details, see our Privacy Policy.

Why is the app not generating a response?

Please make sure you have a stable internet connection. If the issue persists, restarting the app or checking for updates in your device's App Store usually resolves connectivity issues. If you still encounter problems, feel free to contact us.
