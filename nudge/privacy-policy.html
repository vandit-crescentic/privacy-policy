Privacy Policy for Nudge

Last updated: March 25, 2026

Overview

Nudge ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

Information We Collect

Conversation Data & Well-being Information

  • Your chat messages with the AI assistant, which may include reflections on your thoughts, mood, and mental well-being
  • AI responses generated for you based on this information
  • This data is processed to provide the service and help you manage overthinking

Health and Well-being Data

Nudge is designed to help users manage overthinking and improve mental well-being. To provide this service, the application accesses, collects, and processes information related to your mental and emotional state that you share during interactions with our AI assistant.

We collect and use this health and well-being data (including patterns of thought and mood-related reflections) for the following purposes:

  • To provide personalized AI-driven guidance and support
  • To help you identify and overcome specific overthinking patterns
  • To enhance the relevance of the "Nudge" service for your mental well-being

This information is processed through our secure servers and via the Anthropic Claude API to provide you with the core functionality of the app. We do not sell your health data or use it for marketing or advertising purposes.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide and maintain the Nudge service
  • Improve the AI assistant's responses
  • Send you daily check-in notifications (if enabled)
  • Provide customer support

Data Storage and Security

  • Conversations are stored locally on your device
  • Some data is processed through our secure servers to provide AI responses
  • We use industry-standard encryption for data in transit
  • We do not sell your personal information

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Anthropic Claude API: For AI conversation processing

Each of these services has their own privacy policy.

Data Retention

  • Local conversation data remains on your device until you delete it
  • Server-side processing is ephemeral and not permanently stored

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your data
  • Delete your data (via app settings)
  • Opt out of notifications

Children's Privacy

Nudge is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy in the app.
